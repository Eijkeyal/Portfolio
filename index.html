a little about me

I’m a software engineer who builds things that work, and keeps going until they feel right.

I started programming because I wanted tools that didn’t exist. That habit never stopped: 64 repositories, 15+ real projects, and a 4-month internship where I learned how it all works on a team, with reviews, deadlines, and code other people have to live with.

I work across the stack, from databases and APIs to the interface on top, with a bit of 3D on the side. It means I understand how the pieces fit together, and it also means I have no one else to blame when something breaks.

I like small teams, clear writing, and code the next person can read without asking me. I care about the small details, and I would always pick simple over clever.
